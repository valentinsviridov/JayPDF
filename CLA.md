# JayPDF Contributor License Policy & Draft Agreement

> **Status: DRAFT / NOT YET ACTIVE**
>
> The automated Contributor License Agreement (CLA) workflow is currently being established and is not yet active. No contributor consent is assumed or fabricated. Until an explicit affirmative acceptance mechanism is available, substantial third-party code contributions cannot be accepted into the commercially relicensable codebase.

---

## 1. Contributor Rights & Purpose

Contributors to JayPDF retain copyright ownership of their contributions.

JayPDF is distributed under a dual-licensing model:
1. Open-source under the **GNU Affero General Public License version 3 only (AGPL-3.0-only)**; and
2. Separate **commercial/proprietary licenses** granted by the project owner, Valentin Sviridov.

To maintain the legal authority to distribute JayPDF under both models, the project requires an explicit, broad license grant from contributors for any substantial code contributions.

---

## 2. Contributor License Grant (When Active)

When an affirmative CLA mechanism is formally activated, contributors will be asked to grant Valentin Sviridov a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to:

- Reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute the contribution and derivative works;
- Distribute the contribution under the AGPL-3.0-only and under separate commercial or proprietary terms without additional contributor approval.

---

## 3. Representations

Contributors must confirm that:
- They are legally entitled to grant the license and that the contribution is their original creation;
- The contribution does not violate any third-party intellectual property rights or trade secrets;
- If their employer has rights to intellectual property that includes the contribution, they have received permission to make contributions on behalf of that employer.

---

## 4. Current Policy

Until an affirmative CLA signing workflow is operational, external contributions are limited to minor bug reports, documentation clarifications, and issue discussions.
