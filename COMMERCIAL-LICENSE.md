# JayPDF Commercial Licensing

JayPDF is available under two licensing options.

## Open-source use

You may use JayPDF under the GNU Affero General Public License
version 3 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`).

See `LICENSES/AGPL-3.0-only.txt`.

## Commercial licensing

A separate commercial license is available for organizations that
want to use JayPDF under terms other than the AGPL-3.0-only.

Typical commercial-license use cases include incorporating JayPDF
or JayPDF-owned components into closed-source or proprietary
products where the organization does not want to comply with the
requirements of the AGPL.

Commercial licenses are granted only for intellectual property
that the JayPDF copyright holder has authority to license.

Third-party components remain subject to their respective licenses.
In particular, portions derived from PDFLince remain subject to the
original MIT license notice contained in
`LICENSES/MIT-PDFLince.txt`.

For commercial licensing, contact the JayPDF project owner,
Valentin Sviridov.
